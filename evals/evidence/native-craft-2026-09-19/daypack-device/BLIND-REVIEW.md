Viewed through `view_image`: M-plans.png, M-checklist.png, M-partial.png, M-summary.png, N-plans.png, N-checklist.png, N-partial.png, N-summary.png. Supplied capture context: Android, 1080 × 2424, font scale 1.0; tool previews were resized to 912 × 2048. Only these eight images were inspected. No source, provenance, prior reports, or other directories were read. Conclusions concern visible composition and wording; captures do not establish interaction behavior, persistence, accessibility, or content below their edges.

- **Task clarity:** Both make choosing an outing and checking packing items clear. N fits all three outing names, descriptions, and counts in its plans capture, alongside the session-retention note. M's large introduction and separate tall cards leave the third outing's content outside the capture. M gives individual choices more breathing room; N supports comparison in one view.
- **Action roles:** N's “Finish packing” names an action directly. M's muted “Everything’s packed” reads more like a status assertion while the counter still says zero or one packed; its nearby finish prerequisite helps explain the intended role. Both distinguish back navigation from the main task. On summary, M clearly prioritizes “Choose another outing” above “Review or unpack items.” N shows review first but gives the partially visible “Start this pack again” a strong filled treatment. That makes restarting visually prominent while the full control is below the capture edge; its behavior cannot be inferred.
- **Feedback in context:** M connects four segmented marks closely to explicit packed/remaining counts and uses a strong row tint plus check in the partial state. N combines count, continuous progress, and remaining text inside a larger status panel, then adds “Packed” to the checked row. N's extra row label makes completion explicit; M communicates the same step more compactly. Both keep undo guidance visible. N's motivational status heading consumes room without adding state information.
- **Typography and coherence:** Both sustain a clear hierarchy of large heavy titles, quieter descriptions, and small uppercase labels across the captures. M's navy and lime identity is bolder; N's green palette and repeated backpack shape feel consistent from entry to completion. N uses the summary's centered celebration to mark a stage change, then returns to left-aligned contents. M preserves left alignment throughout, making the transition more continuous.
- **Content versus decoration:** M's lime lines beside plan counts resemble status tracks although the plans capture supplies no progress meaning for them. Its actual checklist segments have a much clearer job. N's numbered plan badges and compact shared container organize choices efficiently, but its checklist status panel adds enough height to place the finish control near the lower edge. M's simpler checklist brings items and finish control higher with substantial space below.
- **Cross-screen tradeoff:** N is more economical on plan selection and differentiates its summary list from editable checklist rows through plain ticks and tighter spacing. M reuses checkbox-like summary rows, which maintains visual continuity but leaves their apparent interactivity less distinct from the packing screen. M exposes both key summary choices fully; N's large celebration and summary card leave its filled action clipped. Neither set is uniformly more compact or clearer across all stages: N concentrates information effectively on plans, while M uses space more economically during packing and makes the visible summary next step easier to scan.
