Gölcük after the night shift

A bus depot in Kocaeli, a missing spare, and the people who still made the 05:10.

The spare gearbox was listed as on-site. It was in İzmit, in a locked cage, and the only person with the code was on leave. Dispatch did not cancel the 05:10. They borrowed a unit from a coach that would sit until Monday.
